AFRICA TOUR AI is an artificial intelligence-powered tourism platform designed to make exploring Africa easier, smarter and more accessible.

🎯 Our Mission

To help travellers discover Africa, plan their trips, explore destinations and access reliable tourism information through artificial intelligence.

🌍 Our Vision

To become one of Africa’s leading intelligent tourism platforms.

🚀 Main Features

* AI-powered travel assistant
* African destination discovery
* Smart travel budget estimation
* Hotel and accommodation recommendations
* Personalized travel itineraries
* Tourism activities and experiences
* Visa and travel information
* Multilingual assistance

🌐 Our Market

All 54 African countries, with international expansion planned.

💼 Business Model

* Free and Premium subscriptions
* Hotel and travel booking commissions
* Tourism business partnerships
* Professional services

❤️ Our Commitment

Connecting people to the beauty, culture and diversity of Africa through technology.
